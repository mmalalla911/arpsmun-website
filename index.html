About ARPSMUN

Model United Nations, or MUN, is an educational simulation of a United Nations conference organized by high school students in Bahrain. Participants take on roles such as delegates, chairpersons, and press members while discussing solutions to global issues.

This experience helps students develop leadership, critical thinking, diplomacy, negotiation, public speaking, and debate skills. It also provides valuable networking opportunities.

Al Rawabi Private School's previous MUN conference was a remarkable success, featuring notable guests such as H.E. Shikha Fajer bint Ali bin Rashid Al Khalifa and Ms. Nancy Ellie Khadori from the Shura Council. This year, Al Rawabi will host its fifth MUN conference, ARPSMUN V, continuing its mission to empower students and the wider community.

Guiding Principles of ARPSMUN

Mission

To educate young students by raising awareness of global issues. We aim to develop critical thinking, enhance public speaking and diplomatic skills, and build confidence through meaningful engagement. By fostering intellectual growth, we inspire youth to become proactive contributors to a better world.

Vision

To build a respected student-led organization that students aspire to be part of. Through impactful Model United Nations conferences, we aim to shape a generation of informed, confident, and compassionate young leaders with the determination and courage to address global challenges.

Leadership

Encouraging students to lead with confidence and responsibility.

Diplomacy

Promoting respectful communication and peaceful negotiation.

Integrity

Acting with honesty, fairness, and accountability.

Global Awareness

Understanding international issues and diverse perspectives.
